# Privacy Policy for Rage Game Arcade

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Rage Game Arcade ("the app") is developed by SoupFork Studios ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what information the app does and does not collect.

## Summary

Rage Game Arcade does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app does not require an internet connection to play, does not use analytics or tracking software, does not display ads, and does not require you to create an account or sign in.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal data. The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other personal information stored on your device.

## Data Storage

Any game data (such as progress or settings) is stored locally on your own device. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party, and we have no access to it.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Children's Privacy

Rage Game Arcade does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because the app collects no personal data at all, this applies equally to users of all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if a future update adds online features), we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
